---
title: Accessibility
description: What QueryLens has been checked against for accessibility, how each check is run, and what has not been checked yet.
---

QueryLens aims at WCAG 2.2 level AA. This page says what has been checked, how, and what has not. It is **not** a
conformance claim: a conformance statement needs the manual testing listed under *Not yet checked*, and that has not
been done.

## Checked automatically

Fifty-four routes are scanned with [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core) against the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2
rules at levels A and AA. The scan runs in the project's test suite (`e2e/audit.spec.ts`), with reduced motion on. It
covers the landing page, the playground, every tool and comparison, every docs page, the not-found page, and one
stage, one lesson and one challenge page standing for their kinds. The last run found no violations on any of them.
The other stage, lesson and challenge pages are built from the same components, but they are not scanned one by one.

Automated scanning catches only part of WCAG: a pass means no *detectable* violation, not that a page is accessible.

## Checked by hand, in the browser

- **Keyboard, in the tree diagrams.** The parse tree, the query plan and the B+Tree are each one tab stop. The arrow
  keys move to the parent, to the first child, and left and right through siblings, and the view follows focus. Each
  node is announced with its level and its place among its siblings.
- **Tables for the simulators.** The hash index, LSM tree, join order and columnar storage each have a *Show as table*
  control that shows the same numbers as a table, for readers who cannot use the diagram.

## Not yet checked

- **Screen readers.** Nothing has yet been tested with NVDA, VoiceOver or TalkBack. Until it has, the claims above are
  about the markup, not about what a screen reader says aloud.
- **Keyboard through every page.** The tree diagrams and the table views were checked by hand. The rest of the
  keyboard flow was not walked end to end.
- **Colour in every theme.** The automated contrast checks cover the themes, but a person has not reviewed the
  charts and the highlights for colour-vision differences.

## Known limits

- The B+Tree's leaf sibling chain is drawn but not walkable with the arrow keys, which follow the tree only.
- The step narration is text, but a few visuals carry their meaning in colour as well as in their labels.

## Reporting a problem

Tell us where, what you were doing, and what happened, on the project's
[issue tracker](https://github.com/Naveen-Boddepalli/query-lens/issues). Please say which assistive technology you
used, if any.
